**Thandi, PhD candidate, Civil Engineering.** Thandi logs flow and water
quality data from field instruments into Excel, one workbook per field trip.
She asks a chat model to summarise them and has noticed that its answers
change between runs. She wants to know which answer to trust, and how to
describe the AI use in her thesis.

**Rajesh, MSc student, Electrical Engineering.** Rajesh is about to collect
sensor data for a machine-learning model. He has not yet set up a folder
structure or decided on file names, and assumes he can clean the data later.
He needs to see what "later" costs.

**Dr Mokoena, lecturer.** Dr Mokoena keeps class marks and project
allocations in spreadsheets and would like an AI tool to tidy them. She is
unsure whether that is allowed under POPIA.

**Ms Pillay, School administrator.** Ms Pillay maintains registration and
progression records shared across several staff. Each person enters names
and dates differently. She wants a layout and a drop-down list that stop the
errors at entry.
